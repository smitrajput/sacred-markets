# Status and roadmap

As of 28 November 2026, USDC Earn and 2x cirBTC Trade are live as an Arc Testnet demo. The app, read API, deployment records and four MVP contracts are in [sacred-markets](https://github.com/smitrajput/sacred-markets).

## Current scope

The demo uses test assets, a Uniswap v4 venue and an operator-maintained price guard. Independent audits, production oracles and final Shariah approval remain pending. Preliminary scholar feedback has identified fixes to address.

## Planned

- Audited margin Trade and Earn pilot, with funded protection and measured demand.
- SIFT, higher leverage tiers and mobile onboarding as their release gates are met.
- Borrow after its separate pricing, collateral and Shariah questions are resolved.
- 1inch Aqua shared-liquidity integration, with atomic cash reservations and explicit product accounting.
- Term mode after margin mode and the corresponding Shariah approvals.

The 100x capital-efficiency goal is an ambition without a validated benchmark. Existing economic models include no Aqua uplift. The earlier term/token implementation is retained under `protocol/` for historical reference.
